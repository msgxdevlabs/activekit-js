New Vue 3 binding: `createActiveKit` app plugin, `provideActiveKit` for
subtree-scoped clients, `useActiveKit` and `useProgress` composables, and the
`ActiveKitWidget` component. Lifecycle glue over the shared widget in
`@activekit/js`, like every binding — no logic of its own, read-only by
construction.

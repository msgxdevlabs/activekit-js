Questions we get

Read the docs
What counts as a credit?

Whatever your ledger already calls one. ActiveKit meters the payout, so a credit in your product is a credit here, at your own unit price.

What happens when I hit the monthly cap?

Campaigns pause instead of overspending, and the ledger records the attempted payouts so you can replay them after raising the cap.

Do drafts and sandbox runs cost anything?

No. Draft campaigns, replays against past events, and sandbox ledgers are unmetered on every plan, including Starter.

Can I move a campaign between workspaces?

Yes, and the ledger history moves with it, because a campaign is a versioned object rather than a dashboard setting.

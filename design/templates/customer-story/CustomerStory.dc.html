Customer story

Parseq replaced three growth scripts with one ledger.

Parseq sells an AI research assistant metered in credits. Their referral, streak, and win-back offers each lived in a different cron job, and none of them agreed with finance. Four days after installing the SDK, all three ran as campaigns against the production balance.

The problem was reconciliation, not growth

Parseq already knew referrals worked. What they could not do was tell finance how much a referral cost, because the grant happened in one service, the fraud check in another, and the reversal by hand.

Moving the logic into campaign objects meant every grant carried a reason, a cap, and a version. The finance export stopped being an argument.

"We deleted the referral cron job and the spreadsheet that checked it. The ledger is the answer now."
Dana Whitlock, Staff Engineer at Parseq

What they shipped in week one

Three campaigns, each with a cap that pauses rather than overspends, and a sandbox replay of the previous quarter to check the numbers before anything paid out.

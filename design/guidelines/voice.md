# Voice

ActiveKit copy follows the house voice principles in `uploads/voice-principles.md`. This file applies them to product and marketing surfaces.

## The five rules that change copy the most

1. **Key point first, context second.** A claim, then the mechanism. "Growth loops that pay out. Launch referral, streak, and win-back campaigns against your own credit ledger, no growth engineer required."
2. **No em dashes.** Use a comma, a colon, or a full stop. This applies to UI strings, headlines, table labels, and docs.
3. **Never use** "dive into", "game-changing", "straightforward", "leverage", "synergize", "circle back", "touch base".
4. **Don't hedge.** Cut "I think" and "I believe" when the sentence is already a statement.
5. **Vary sentence length,** and join clauses with "so", "because", or "for example" instead of stacking fragments.

## Voice on ActiveKit surfaces

- **Person:** talk to the reader as *you*. The product is *ActiveKit*, not *we*. Write "Point it at a credit balance", not "We let you point it at a credit balance".
- **Casing:** sentence case in headlines, buttons, and nav. All caps only at `micro-cap` (10px) for eyebrows and table headers. No Title Case Headlines.
- **Buttons are verbs, one to three words:** "Start free", "Launch campaign", "Talk to sales", "Read the docs".
- **Section labels are single nouns:** Outcomes. Campaigns. Developers. Pricing.
- **Numbers carry the argument,** so write them precisely: `10,480`, `61.0%`, `1,009,200`. Never "10k+". This is also why numeric cells run `tnum` and `zero`.
- **Bold the key point** in long-form and docs, one idea per paragraph, bullets for lists and prose for explanation.
- **No emoji.** The spec defines no emoji role and the slate/teal palette gives them nowhere to sit.

## Before and after

| Off-voice | In voice |
|---|---|
| A game-changing way to leverage your credit ledger | Turn your credit ledger into a growth loop |
| Let's dive into campaign logic | Campaign logic, in three parts |
| Setup is straightforward | Setup takes two calls |
| Trusted by 10k+ teams shipping AI apps | 10,480 teams run campaigns on ActiveKit |
| We built ActiveKit so that you can grow faster | Launch a growth loop today, ship it before lunch |
| Referral loops, streaks, win-back, all in one place | Referral loops, streaks, and win-back run on one ledger |

## Tone check

If a sentence reads like a corporate memo or like an AI wrote it, rewrite it. The target is a clear, thoughtful colleague talking to a smart friend: professional, specific, unexcited.
